04 / Gate quick guide

ANDOutput is 1 only when both inputs are 1.
OROutput is 1 when at least one input is 1.
NOTReverses the input: 0 becomes 1, and 1 becomes 0.
NAND / NORThese give the opposite output of AND / OR.
XOROutput is 1 when the two inputs are different.
XNOROutput is 1 when the two inputs are the same.
